# Theme artwork

Version 1.11 extends the generated artwork to Woodland, Meadow, Sakura, Autumn and Halloween, and adds a separate hanging sprig for each of the six nature themes. Preview 2 replaces the small sprigs with broad corner garlands: see [current garland prompts](GARLAND-PROMPTS.md). The [seasonal border prompts](SEASONAL-PROMPTS.md) and [original panel sprig prompts](PANEL-PROMPTS.md) record the earlier assets. All assets were created with the built-in ChatGPT image tool with true alpha transparency; the original PNG bytes are retained.

One worker decodes the requested theme only: a half-size frame and half-size garland. The cache holds one theme, not the entire set. Substantial panel corner garlands follow actual dock/widget bounds and are clipped away from text and controls. They do not scroll with dock apps or add focus targets. Off hides all decoration; Art mode hides it with the UI.

## Original Summertime artwork

`summertime.png` is a raster image generated for QuietHome with the built-in ChatGPT image-generation tool, using actual alpha transparency. It is not an SVG or a programmatic botanical drawing. No user wallpaper was used as input. The first generated frame was refined to make its edges much lighter; only the refined asset is shipped.

The original 1.10 implementation decoded a sampled copy on a worker and anchored the four image quadrants at the screen corners without stretching the flowers. Lower clusters include shallow bottom-edge foliage. Light/Lush adjust their size; Off hides them. Art mode hides the artwork. There is no runtime image generation, download or animation.

## Initial generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent raster decorative overlay for an Android TV launcher, not a wallpaper or UI mockup. Create a realistic summertime botanical screen border: sunlit fresh green leaves, delicate white daisies, a few warm yellow wildflowers and blue cornflowers, natural fine stems and detailed photographic petals. Composition: wide landscape 16:9 canvas. Keep ALL flowers and foliage confined to small upper-left and upper-right corner clusters, lower-left and lower-right corners, and a very shallow sparse bottom-edge garland. The central 88% width and central 85% height must be entirely transparent. Top centre must be completely empty for a clock. Side decoration must hug only the outermost edges. Bottom garland should be thin and airy, with gaps, not a thick flower bed. Elegant asymmetry and real botanical detail, soft warm midsummer daylight, believable photographic cutouts with clean semi-transparent edges. Actual alpha transparency everywhere except the plants, including all gaps between leaves and petals; no sky, landscape, soil, backdrop, opaque plate, checkerboard pattern, cast shadow on a background, text, UI, logo or watermark. Not illustration, not vector, not cartoon, not plastic CGI. Designed to frame existing TV wallpaper without covering labels or app tiles.

## Refinement prompt

Edit this transparent summertime botanical frame for a TV interface. Preserve the photorealistic daisies, blue cornflowers, yellow wildflowers and green foliage style and preserve genuine alpha transparency. The current corner clusters are much too large. Make ALL decoration MUCH smaller and thinner: each top corner cluster must fit within the outermost 5 percent of image width and uppermost 12 percent of image height. Lower corner clusters also within outermost 5 percent width and lowest 10 percent height. Make the bottom garland no more than 4 percent of image height, thin and sparse, with clear gaps. Everything else must be completely transparent, including the entire top edge between the tiny corner clusters. Do not simply shrink the transparent canvas; keep the full wide 16:9 canvas and move the smaller plants right to its four edges. No new background, no text, no graphic lines, no cartoon/vector style. Realistic tiny flowers and leaves, with natural fine edges, designed to stay outside screen content that starts 5 percent from the edges. More than 90 percent of the canvas should be fully transparent.
