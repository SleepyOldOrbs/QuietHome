# Seasonal artwork generation

These five transparent PNG assets were generated independently with the built-in ChatGPT image-generation tool using `transparent_background: true`, without private wallpaper inputs. Original alpha is retained. Summertime's earlier prompts remain in README.md.

## woodland

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: genuine alpha-channel raster decorative frame for a television launcher. WIDE landscape 16:9 transparent canvas. Woodland: fresh realistic English ivy, small oak leaves, delicate fern tips and fine trailing vines. Lush natural green botanical photography.. All objects must be tiny and confined to the four corners and a very shallow sparse bottom edge. Each top corner cluster within outermost 7 percent width and top 13 percent height; bottom corners within outermost 9 percent width and bottom 10 percent height. Sparse bottom foliage no more than 4 percent height, with broad transparent gaps. At least 90 percent of image must be fully transparent. Entire centre and top edge between corners completely transparent. Realistic photographic cutout textures, delicate natural edges, tasteful asymmetry. No landscape, background, shadow plate, checkerboard, text, UI, frames, borders made of graphic lines, vector graphics, illustration or cartoon. Actual transparency in every gap. Make a light restrained decorative frame, never a thick floral border. This will be composited over an existing wallpaper and must not obscure controls.
